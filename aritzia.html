01 — Campaign Brief

Warmth without
noise.

Aritzia is one of Canada's most quietly powerful fashion brands. Their aesthetic is studied restraint — never loud, never overselling, always letting the product speak. This Spring 2025 campaign, "The Effortless Edit," launches their new collection across five digital surfaces with one creative brief: warmth without noise.

The spring palette — blush, sage, dusty lilac, warm cream — lives in the clothes and the creative. The campaign itself stays rooted in Aritzia's signature black and warm neutral. The brand doesn't need to shout spring. It shows it.

"Aritzia customers don't want to be sold to. They want to be understood. Every word in this campaign is a design decision."

The Insight
Aritzia's customer edits her wardrobe, not her identity.
The campaign doesn't say "shop now." It says "this is your wardrobe, considered." The CTA is always secondary to the feeling. The clothes are the hero — the campaign gets out of the way.
The Strategy
One campaign. Five surfaces. One voice.
Email anchors with storytelling. Instagram creates desire through visual restraint. Stories drive urgency. Display drives traffic. Paid social retargets with product focus. Each surface has a distinct role — all speak in the same voice.

the clothes.

03 — Email Campaign

The highest-intent
surface.

Email is opened by people who already love the brand. The design rewards that relationship with editorial storytelling — not a product grid. Single column, maximum 500px width, editorial copy, one call to action. No discounts. No countdown timers. Just the collection, considered.

Design decisions
Single-column layout. Hero image carries the emotion — copy supports it. CTA as a full-width black block, not a button. Secondary blocks use spring palette colours. No product grid — that's for the website, not the email.
Copy strategy
No exclamation points. No "SALE" in caps. No emojis. The copy is quiet and confident — the same register as the brand's Instagram captions. Every word earns its place or it's cut.

06 — What I Learned

Restraint is
a creative choice.

1
The brand voice is the campaign.
Aritzia doesn't use exclamation points. They don't shout SALE. That restraint is the brand identity. Designing this campaign meant internalising that voice before writing a single word — then letting it drive every decision across five surfaces.
2
Every surface has a different job.
Email builds relationship. Instagram builds desire. Stories build urgency. Display drives traffic. Paid social closes the loop. The same message, five completely different executions — each calibrated to where the user is in their journey.
3
Copy is design.
"Shop now" and "Explore the collection" are the same instruction with completely different feelings. Every line in this campaign — the subject line, the display headline, the story swipe prompt — is a design decision as deliberate as any visual choice.
